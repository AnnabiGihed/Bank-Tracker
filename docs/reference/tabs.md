# Tabs

Pick a month at the top, with ‹ › or the drop-down. Every tab follows it (`?m=yyyy-MM` in the address).

| Tab | What it shows | What you can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Income, spending (fixed and variable), savings, what is left, the last months, spending by category, fixed payments still to come, the biggest expenses. A banner tells you when something needs your attention. | Select a category in the chart to see its transactions. |
| **Fixed payments** | Suggestions found in your history, then your accepted fixed payments with their status this month: paid, due, late or amount changed. | ✓ accepts a suggestion, ✕ dismisses it. ✎ edits the name, category, amount and rhythm (monthly, quarterly, yearly). |
| **Transactions** | The month's transactions. A dashed category is automatic and not confirmed yet. | Search and filter. Change a category: it applies to every payment of that payee, with an "Only this one" option. ✓ confirms an automatic category. ☆ marks the payee as a fixed payment. |
| **To review** | Every payee whose automatic category you have not confirmed. | Confirm one by one, or all shown at once. |
| **Categories** | Your categories with their totals per month. | Create, edit or delete a category: name, colour, keywords, and how it counts (spending, income, savings, own transfer). |
| **Settings** | The bank connection, the stored history and the data folder. | Reconnect the bank. Export every transaction as comma-separated values (CSV). |
