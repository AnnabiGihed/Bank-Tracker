# Reconnect the bank

Your consent to read the account lasts 180 days. The app warns you 15 days before it ends. After it ends, syncs
fail until you reconnect.

1. Open the **Settings** tab.
1. Choose **Reconnect** and log in at KBC with itsme or a card reader.
1. Wait for the download to finish.

Your history and your decisions (fixed payments, categories) are kept: reconnecting only renews the access.
