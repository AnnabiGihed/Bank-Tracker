# Track a debt

Follow a bank loan or money borrowed from a person: when it is repaid and how far you are.

## Add a debt

1. Open the **Income** tab and find the credit that brought the money in.
1. Set its category to **Loan received** (bank loan) or **Borrowed from people** (a person). The repayment plan form
   opens.
1. Fill in the lender, the amount received and the day you borrowed it. Add the lender's IBAN for a person: repayments
   are found by IBAN, or by name without one.
1. Choose the plan: one payment on a date, or instalments with a start date, a frequency and a day of the month. Give
   the amount per instalment or the number of instalments; the other is worked out.

You can skip the form and add the debt later on the **Debts** tab, also when the credit is older than the 90 days the
bank returns.

## Follow it

The **Debts** tab shows each debt's schedule, the repayments matched so far and the progress. The Overview warns when a
debt is due soon, overdue or has no plan.

- A bank loan is matched through its instalments (the `LOAN:` series); a person through transfers to the lender.
- Only payments on or after the day borrowed count, oldest first, until the debt is covered. A payment repays one debt.
- Correct the matching by adding a payment by hand (it always counts) or excluding one (it never counts).

## What it does to your totals

Repayments matched to a debt and bank-loan instalments are not spending, unless you split the payment or chose its
category yourself. Money borrowed is not income. See
[How payments are classified](../explanation/payment-classification.md).
